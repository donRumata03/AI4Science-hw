# L2: eight NCM claims

[generated] Derived from [L1](L1.md). Page references concern the pinned original
PDF, not prior chat summaries. Source-review outcomes are in [verification](verification.md).

1. **C1 - Learned hierarchy.** Increasing-length code levels encode residual refinements;
   the final level has text length. This is a learned representation, not a vocabulary
   cluster tree. Source: p.6, Section 3.2, Equation 3.
2. **C2 - Within-level factorization.** NCM samples current-level codes independently
   conditional on the cumulative reconstruction. This defines the model; independence
   of the real distribution is not established. Source: p.6, Equation 5; check C2.
3. **C3 - Reconstruction condition.** Each level quantizes a downsampled residual and
   adds its mapped contribution to the reconstruction used by subsequent levels.
   Source: p.6, Equations 7-8; check C3.
4. **C4 - Informative prefixes.** Nested dropout trains reconstruction from sampled
   level prefixes. Useful early codes are encouraged, but an easily generated prior
   is not guaranteed. Source: p.7, Equation 9; check C4.
5. **C5 - Two-stage training/order.** The tokenizer is frozen for generator training;
   training predicts all levels in one pass, while inference generates levels in order.
   Source: p.7, Section 3.4 and Algorithms 1-2; check C5.
6. **C6 - Corrupted conditions.** Generator training can replace conditioning codes
   while retaining clean target codes. Changing this is a separate experimental choice.
   Source: p.7, Conditioning Corruption and Equation 10.
7. **C7 - Dataset-specific reconstruction.** Table 1 reports 99.86% for LM1B and 99.99%
   for OWT. Neither is a measurement reproduced here or a generation-quality guarantee.
   Source: p.8, Table 1; check C7.
8. **C8 - Evaluation scope.** GenPPL and mean per-sample unigram entropy measure a
   quality/diversity trade-off; NFE must be supplemented with wall time. Corpus
   diversity and MAUVE need a separately pinned protocol. Source: p.9, Section 4.2,
   and p.11, Section 4.6; check C8 verifies the p.9 evaluation statement.
